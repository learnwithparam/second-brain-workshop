Subject: How we cut agent mistakes by half

Hi team,

Last quarter a client asked us why their support agent kept giving wrong refund answers.
We did not change the model. We added a 30-case test list that runs before every release.
Two releases were stopped in the first month. Mistakes dropped from 12 percent to 6 percent.

Their CTO now approves each prompt change in a pull request, like code.
