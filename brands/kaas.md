# kaas

Who reads it: CTOs and founders who pay for AI work that must run in production.

## Good
- Names the cost, the risk or the time saved.
- Shows how a team checks the work before it ships.
- Comes from a real client job, with the client's name removed.

## Bad
- A toy demo that never meets real data.
- A claim about speed with no number.
- Advice that skips who approves the change.
