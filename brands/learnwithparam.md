# learnwithparam

Who reads it: engineers, engineering managers and product managers who build with AI agents.

## Good
- Shows how the system is built, with a diagram or a real file.
- Gives a number or a result you can check.
- Teaches one idea a reader can try in a day.

## Bad
- Hype with no design behind it ("AI will change everything").
- A list of prompts with no reason why they work.
- A tool demo that has no failure case.
